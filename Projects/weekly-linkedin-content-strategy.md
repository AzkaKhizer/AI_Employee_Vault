# Weekly LinkedIn Content Strategy

**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Pending_Approval
**Processed:** 2026-02-17 08:58

## Objective

Launch a weekly LinkedIn content strategy to grow brand presence within 60 days.

## Assumptions

- Team size: 1-2 people (content creator + reviewer)
- Budget: Organic only (no paid promotion assumed)
- Starting from low or no existing LinkedIn cadence

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Consistent weekly posting cadence established by Day 14
- [ ] Minimum 4 content pillars defined and rotating
- [ ] Engagement rate increase of 25%+ over baseline by Day 60
- [ ] Follower growth of 15%+ over baseline by Day 60

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–7)

**Milestone:** Content pillars defined, posting calendar created.

- [ ] Audit current LinkedIn profile and recent post performance
- [ ] Define 4 content pillars (e.g., thought leadership, behind-the-scenes, industry insights, team highlights)
- [ ] Create a 60-day content calendar with weekly themes
- [ ] Establish brand voice and visual guidelines for LinkedIn
- [ ] Set up baseline metrics (current followers, avg engagement rate, impressions)

**Dependencies:** None
**Risk:** Unclear brand voice delays content creation — mitigate by timboxing voice guide to 1 page.

## Phase 2 — Launch (Days 8–21)

**Milestone:** First 2 weeks of posts published on schedule.

- [ ] Draft and schedule Week 1 posts (minimum 3 posts)
- [ ] Draft and schedule Week 2 posts (minimum 3 posts)
- [ ] Test posting times (morning vs. midday vs. evening)
- [ ] Engage with 10+ relevant accounts daily (comments, shares)
- [ ] Track impressions and engagement per post

**Dependencies:** Phase 1 complete
**Risk:** Low initial engagement discourages continuation — mitigate by setting realistic early benchmarks.

## Phase 3 — Optimize (Days 22–42)

**Milestone:** Top-performing content format identified, engagement trending up.

- [ ] Analyze Weeks 1-2 performance data
- [ ] Double down on highest-engagement content format
- [ ] Introduce one new format (carousel, poll, video, or article)
- [ ] Refine posting schedule based on analytics
- [ ] Begin employee advocacy — encourage team to reshare posts
- [ ] Draft and schedule Weeks 4-6 posts

**Dependencies:** Phase 2 performance data
**Risk:** Analytics insufficient to draw conclusions — mitigate by ensuring UTM tracking and LinkedIn Analytics access.

## Phase 4 — Scale & Report (Days 43–60)

**Milestone:** Sustained cadence proven, 60-day results compiled.

- [ ] Maintain weekly posting cadence (3+ posts/week)
- [ ] Compile 60-day performance report (followers, engagement, impressions, top posts)
- [ ] Document repeatable content workflow for ongoing execution
- [ ] Identify next-phase opportunities (paid promotion, LinkedIn newsletters, partnerships)
- [ ] Present results and recommend go-forward strategy

**Dependencies:** Phase 3 complete
**Risk:** Results plateau — mitigate by having 2-3 experimental content ideas ready.
