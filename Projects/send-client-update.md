# Send Client Update

**Priority:** High
**Status:** Pending_Approval
**Processed:** 2026-02-17 09:08

## Objective

Send email to client updating them about project completion and request final payment.

## Assumptions

- Client contact info is known internally
- Project deliverables are already completed and handed over
- Payment terms were previously agreed upon

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Client update email drafted and reviewed
- [ ] Final payment request included with correct amount and payment details
- [ ] Email sent to client
- [ ] Payment confirmation received or follow-up scheduled

---

## Phase 1 — Preparation (Day 1)

**Milestone:** Email draft ready for review.

- [ ] Gather project completion details (deliverables list, completion date, key outcomes)
- [ ] Confirm final invoice amount and payment terms
- [ ] Draft client update email covering project status and payment request
- [ ] Attach any supporting documents (final deliverable summary, invoice)

**Dependencies:** None
**Risk:** Missing deliverable details delays draft — mitigate by checking project records first.

## Phase 2 — Review & Send (Day 1–2)

**Milestone:** Email sent to client.

- [ ] Internal review of email draft (tone, accuracy, completeness)
- [ ] Send email to client
- [ ] Log send date and recipient for audit trail

**Dependencies:** Phase 1 complete
**Risk:** Email bounces or goes to spam — mitigate by confirming email address beforehand.

## Phase 3 — Follow-Up (Days 3–7)

**Milestone:** Payment confirmed or escalation initiated.

- [ ] Monitor for client response within 48 hours
- [ ] If no response, send a polite follow-up reminder
- [ ] Once payment received, confirm and close out project billing
- [ ] Archive all correspondence

**Dependencies:** Phase 2 complete
**Risk:** Client disputes amount — mitigate by referencing original agreement in the email.
